Licensing & Compliance

A better relationship with your licensing agency.

When compliance is always in order, inspections become a confidence boost — not a stressful scramble. Daycare Portal keeps you ready year-round.

  • 1

    Generate an audit link

    One click creates a time-limited, read-only link scoped to compliance data only. No login required for the inspector.

  • 2

    Inspector reviews remotely

    Immunization records, staff certifications, attendance logs, emergency contacts, and compliance forms — all in one view.

  • 3

    Physical visit becomes a formality

    When the inspector does visit, the paperwork review is already done. They walk the facility, sign off, and leave in under an hour.

  • 4

    Link expires automatically

    No permanent access granted. Every audit link has an expiry date. Security and compliance baked in.
