# Feature 10 — Billing & Payments

**Release:** R1  
**Priority:** P1 for R1

---

## Overview
Invoice parents for tuition and fees. Accept online payments. Track what's paid and what's overdue.

---

## Tuition Plans
- Create tuition plans: weekly / bi-weekly / monthly
- Per room / age group pricing
- Part-time vs full-time rates
- Assign a plan to each enrolled child

## Invoice Generation
- Auto-generate invoices on schedule (e.g. 1st of each month)
- Include: tuition, registration fee, supply fee, late pickup fee
- One-off manual invoice for special charges

## Online Payments
- Parent pays from their portal via ACH (bank transfer) or card (Stripe integration)
- Payment confirmation email/notification

## Payment Tracking
- Dashboard: total billed this month, total collected, outstanding balance
- Per-family balance view
- Overdue flagging (past due date)
- Late fee auto-apply (configurable days past due)

## Subsidy / Scholarship
- Mark a child as receiving subsidy (government program)
- Adjust invoice to reflect subsidy amount
- Track subsidy payments separately

## Receipts & Exports
- Downloadable PDF receipt per payment
- Monthly revenue report (CSV)
- End-of-year summary per family (for tax purposes)
