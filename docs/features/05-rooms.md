# Feature 5 — Classroom / Room Management

**Release:** MVP  
**Priority:** P1

---

## Overview
Organize children and staff into rooms (age groups). Each room operates as a mini unit with its own attendance, daily reports, and ratio tracking.

---

## Room Setup
- Room name (e.g. "Infants", "Toddlers", "Pre-K", "School Age")
- Age range (e.g. 6 weeks – 12 months)
- Capacity (max children allowed)
- Ratio rule (staff:children)
- Assigned staff (lead + assistants)

## Room Dashboard (staff view)
- List of all children enrolled in this room
- Today's attendance status for each child
- Quick-add daily report entry per child
- Current ratio indicator

## Enrollment Management
- Move a child from one room to another (with effective date)
- Waitlist per room (if at capacity)

## Multi-Room View (admin)
- Grid view of all rooms with: occupancy, ratio status, staff on duty
- Click into any room for detail
