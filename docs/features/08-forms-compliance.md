# Feature 8 — Forms & Compliance

**Release:** MVP  
**Priority:** P2 (needed before first real enrollment)

---

## Overview
Digital forms replace paper packets. Track signatures, incidents, and maintain audit trail for licensing inspectors.

---

## Enrollment Forms
- Standard enrollment agreement (PDF template rendered in-app)
- Parent/guardian digital signature (typed name + timestamp = legally valid in most US states)
- Health history form
- Media release (photo/video)
- Emergency authorization form

## Incident / Injury Reports
- Date, time, location within facility
- Child involved
- Description of what happened
- Injury type (if any): bruise, cut, bite, fall, etc.
- Action taken
- Was parent notified? Y/N — timestamp of notification
- Staff witness signatures
- PDF export for filing

## Permission Slips
- Create a custom permission slip for events (field trips, activities)
- Send to parents of enrolled children
- Track who has signed / who hasn't (follow-up reminder in R1)

## Audit Trail
- All form actions logged: created, viewed, signed, modified
- Exportable log for licensing inspections
- Document retention: configurable (default 3 years)

## Standard Reports for Licensing
- Monthly attendance summary
- Staff-to-child ratio logs (daily)
- Incident report log

---

## Notes
> US daycare licensing requirements vary by state. The forms and retention rules above are common across most states but should be reviewed against your state's childcare licensing regulations.
