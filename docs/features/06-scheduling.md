# Feature 6 — Scheduling

**Release:** MVP  
**Priority:** P1

---

## Overview
Manage the daycare calendar, child attendance schedules, and staff schedules.

---

## Daycare Calendar
- Operating hours (open/close time by day of week)
- Holidays and closures (mark days closed)
- Special events (field trips, picture day, etc.)
- Visible to all staff and parents (read-only for parents)

## Child Schedule
- Which days of the week each child attends (M/T/W/Th/F)
- Part-time vs full-time designation
- Schedule change requests (parent submits, admin approves)
- Used to pre-populate expected attendance for the day

## Staff Schedule
- Weekly schedule per staff member (days + hours)
- Simple view: who is working each day and in which room

## Substitute / Coverage Alerts
- Flag a day when a staff member calls out
- Alert admin to arrange coverage before ratio is impacted
