# Feature 4 — Staff Management

**Release:** MVP  
**Priority:** P1

---

## Overview
Manage who works at the daycare, their roles, room assignments, and daily attendance.

---

## Staff Profiles
- Full name, photo
- Role: Admin | Lead Teacher | Assistant Teacher | Aide | Director
- Email (used for login)
- Phone
- Hire date
- Certifications (CPR, first aid — upload doc)
- Status: Active | Inactive

## Room Assignment
- Assign one or more staff members to a room
- Designate lead teacher per room

## Staff Check-In / Check-Out
- Staff clock in/out each day (same PIN or QR mechanism as child check-in)
- Used for payroll reference and ratio compliance

## Staff-to-Child Ratio Monitor
- Each room shows current staff count vs. children present
- Color-coded warning: Green (compliant) | Yellow (approaching limit) | Red (over ratio)
- Ratio rules configurable by age group (e.g. infants 1:4, toddlers 1:6, preschool 1:10)

---

## User Roles Summary
| Role | Capabilities |
|---|---|
| Director / Admin | Full access to all features |
| Lead Teacher | Manage their room: attendance, daily reports, child notes |
| Assistant / Aide | Log daily report entries only |
| Parent | Child profile, daily reports, billing (R1) |
