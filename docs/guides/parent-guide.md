# Parent — User Guide

**How to get access:** Your daycare admin will send you an invite link (`/register?token=xxx`).  
**Redirects to:** /parent (your child's portal)

---

## Getting started

1. Open your invite link (from admin or staff)
2. Fill in: **User ID** (your login username), First/Last name, Email (optional), Phone, Password
3. Account is active immediately — no email verification needed
4. Log in at `/login` with your User ID (or email) + password

---

## Logging in
At the login screen, enter either:
- Your **User ID** (e.g. `arudeepa.kumar`) — set during registration
- Your **Email address** — if you provided one

---

## Parent Portal
After login you see your child's card with today's activity feed. The portal has three quick links at the bottom: **Weekly Menu**, **Calendar**, and **Stories**.

Click your child's name/card to open their full profile. If you have multiple children, tap their name to switch between them.

---

## Your child's profile
Click your child's name to open their profile.

**Profile tab** — view personal info, allergies, address. You can edit personal info and address.

**Contacts tab** — view all contacts linked to your child. To add a new pickup person:
1. Click **+ Add pickup**
2. Fill in their name, phone, relationship type
3. Set pickup dates if temporary (e.g., grandparent visiting for a week)
4. Upload their photo (required for pickup authorization)
5. Click **Generate Invite URL** → copy link → share it with them so they can register

**Immunizations tab** — view your child's vaccination status against the CDC schedule. You can enter/edit dates, mark doses as Skipped, or mark a vaccine as Exempt. Use ⚙ Immunization Settings to show/hide vaccines.

**Daily Activities tab** — see what your child did today: meals eaten, nap duration, mood, activities. Select a date to browse past days.

**Documents tab** — link to Paperwork.

---

## Check-In / Check-Out (at front desk)
When you arrive or leave:
1. **Scan** the QR code displayed at the front desk
2. **Enter your check-in code** (shown on your Home page under "My Check-in Code" — set it yourself or ask your admin)
3. See your child's current status (Present / Not recorded)
4. Tap **✓ Check In** or **→ Check Out**

If you have multiple children, all are shown with checkboxes — select which ones to check in/out together.

## My Check-in Code (Portal Home)
Your personal check-in code is shown on the **Home** page of the parent portal.

- The code is **yours** — it stays the same even if your child moves to a different school or room.
- It is **globally unique** across the system — no two people share the same code.
- Default: masked (dots). Click the eye icon to reveal it.
- To change it: type a new 4–6 digit code in the "Change code" field and click **Save**.
  - If the code is already in use by someone else, you'll see an error — choose a different one.
- Staff can use this code at the kiosk to check your child in or out on your behalf.
